## Morning (07:19, late)

- Snoozed my real alarm twice. Opened the app while running to the bathroom, because the sun icon on my home screen reminded me.
- This time the screen was **normal**: no Night-ritual card. Just `ตื่นแล้ว — ลุกจากเตียง · เป้าหมาย 06:30`, and below it `บล็อกปัจจุบัน ตื่น` with the first 10 minutes "ออกจากห้องนอน + เปิดม่าน / รับแสง". That's how I expected yesterday to look.
- I pressed it and got: **"ตื่น 07:19 บันทึกแล้ว รอบนี้ไม่ได้ตั๋ว"**. Honestly, that's the right tone. No red, no "you failed", no lost streak. Just no ticket. I didn't feel bad, and I'd still press it tomorrow.
- The pill top-right now says **1** (streak 1 day). So yesterday counted. It went up even though I woke late. Good, and a bit surprising: what does the streak count, then?
- "อีก 6 วันสตรีคจะปลดล็อกชิ้นต่อไป" means something unlocks at a 7-day streak. I don't know what "ชิ้น" is (a piece of what?), but OK.
- `ถัดไป: โฟกัส · 07:30`: in real life 07:30–09:00 is me on the BTS. The "work day" preset made that a Focus block. I won't change it, too much effort.
