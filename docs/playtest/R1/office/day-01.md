## What I did, in my words

- The login screen opened in **English** ("Welcome to 24/7") even though my phone is Thai. I had to find `ไทย` myself. Small, but it's the very first thing I see.
- Code by email was fine. When I went to Mail and came back, the page still waited for the code ("เช็กอีเมล office@playtest.invalid เพื่อดูรหัสเข้าสู่ระบบ"), so I didn't have to start again. Good.
- After login I didn't get the setup straight away. I landed on Today with a card "ตั้งค่า 2 นาทีให้ 24/7 เป็นของคุณ" and two buttons, `ตั้งค่าเลย` / `ไว้ทีหลัง`. I tapped `ตั้งค่าเลย` because the wake time said **"เป้าหมาย 10:00"** and I wake at 6:30.
- Step 1 `ชีวิตประจำวันแบบไหน?`: **`ทำงานประจำ — ทำงาน จ–ศ 9–18 น.`** That's literally me. Best moment of the day, 1 tap.
- Step 2 `ตารางแต่ละแบบวัน`: the preset gave me `นอน 23:30 → ตื่น 07:00`. The quick chips at the top (`23:00–07:00`, `00:00–08:00`, …) have no 06:30, so I had to open the `วันทำงาน` card and type the times myself.
- Step 3 `10 นาทีแรกของคุณ`: I didn't read it. There's a big button `✓ ใช้ที่แนะนำทั้งหมด` that already has a tick, so I couldn't tell if I still had to press it. I pressed `เสร็จแล้ว` and hoped.
- Step 4 is just `เรียบร้อย` with another `เสร็จแล้ว`. An extra tap for nothing.
- Back on Today: a tip, a reward card "รางวัลการตั้งค่า เมล็ดสตรอว์เบอร์รี 2 เมล็ด · ตั๋วตู้กาชา 1 ใบ", and a checklist "ตั้งค่า 24/7 · ตั้งค่าแล้ว 2 จาก 7". I tapped `ข้ามทิปทั้งหมด`. When I came back to the page the reward card was gone. I never pressed its `โอเค`, so I'm not sure I got anything.

## Hesitations / confusing bits

1. **The wake target still said "เป้าหมาย 10:00" right after I set 06:30.** I thought it hadn't saved. (Next morning it said 06:30, so it was only tonight's day that kept 10:00.)
2. At 9 pm the biggest green button on the screen is **"ตื่นแล้ว — ลุกจากเตียง"**. Why would I press "I'm up" at night?
3. Time fields show **"11:30 PM" / "07:00 AM"** (12-hour) inside a Thai UI. Thais say 23:00.
4. On my small screen the day chips in `วันทำงาน` get cut off: you can see `จ อ พ พฤ ศ ส` and half of `อา`. The `07:00 AM` box touches the right edge.
5. `✓ ใช้ที่แนะนำทั้งหมด`: is it on or not? The tick says done, but it looks like a button.
6. The last screen says "แก้ได้ทุกเมื่อที่ **More** → ตั้งค่า", but the tab is called **`เพิ่มเติม`**.
7. The setup checklist (`/more#setup`) lists "เลือกภาษา 5 เหรียญ" as not done. I already chose ไทย on the login screen.
8. Before setup, at 21:01 the "current block" said `งาน` (work). At 9 pm? That was the default, but it's the first thing a new person sees.
9. Words like `Down Time`, `Night ritual`, `Sunrise` stay in English in the Thai UI. OK for me, but my mum wouldn't get them.

- It really was about 2 minutes.
- The code screen survives switching to the Mail app.
- Cute sprout icon and soft colours. It doesn't look like a work app.
