- Under it, again: `23:00 ไปนอน — กดเริ่มตอนล้มตัวลงนอน ตรงเวลา = ภายใน 10 นาทีจากเวลานอน — เริ่ม`. At breakfast. Streak pill: **0**.

## Night — didn't open

- **Why:** Friday-ish feeling, drinks with friends, home late. I also had no reason to: tomorrow's plan is the same as always, and the app never showed me anything that changes when I close the day.
- **What would have brought me back:** (1) a notification. (2) Something waiting for me: "your radish is ready to pick 🥕", "9 tickets → spin the capsule". (3) The ritual auto-copying today's plan if I don't open it, so skipping costs nothing and opening is a bonus.

- `สมุดเมล็ด 0 จาก 20`, `0 เหรียญ`, decorations at 30–300 coins (I've earned 0 coins all week).
