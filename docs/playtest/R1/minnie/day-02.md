## Confusing / annoying
1. **"ตื่น 07:15 บันทึกแล้ว รอบนี้ไม่ได้ตั๋ว"**: I was 15 minutes after my target and got nothing, and it didn't say *why* or what the window is. Is it ±10? ±15? I'd like "ช่วงได้ตั๋ว 06:45–07:10" printed under the button. As a numbers person, a hidden spec limit drives me crazy.
2. **"ตื่นแล้ว" and the ตื่น block's "เริ่ม" are two different things.** I pressed ตื่นแล้ว, then ticking my water started the ตื่น block as **"เริ่ม 07:15 · เริ่มช้า"**. Then at 23:20, "ดูทั้งวัน" *still* says **"ตื่น — กำลังทำ"**. My wake block has apparently been running for 16 hours.
3. **Ticking the class checklist at 12:15 stamped the Work block "เริ่ม 12:16 · เริ่มช้า".** I was in class at 09:00 on time! I just ticked it afterwards because I don't touch my phone in class. Logging after the fact shouldn't be called "late".
4. **"ดูทั้งวัน" doesn't match my timetable:** "08:00–09:30 โฟกัส — **พลาด**", "09:30–18:30 งาน". My class is **09:00–12:00**, which I typed in setup. So Focus overlaps class, gets marked missed, and my afternoon (gym, free) is "งาน" until 18:30. And my planned gym shows as **"09:30 ออกกำลังกาย 60 นาที"**, i.e. during class. The schedule the app built doesn't respect the class slot I gave it.
5. **My planned tasks never appeared on Today.** Last night I planned "ส่งรายงาน Time Study" (Must, 45 น.) and "อ่าน SPC" for today. The Today screen only showed the 10-min starter and the checklist. I only saw the tasks again in the Night ritual ("จะย้ายไปไหนดี?"). For a *planner*, the plan should be on the Today screen. I did write the report, but I had to go to วางแผน to tick it.
6. **I can't un-tick a done task.** The tick button went grey/disabled. What if I tap it by mistake?
7. Step 2 of the ritual asks for reasons for **"โฟกัส"** and **"Gradation"**, both of which only "failed" because the schedule is wrong (see 4). I tapped "โดนขัดจังหวะ" for all of them, which isn't even true.
8. The template list in ritual step 3 changed its order: now **"วันธรรมดาที่ว่าง 10:00"** is first, and I'm scared one wrong tap gives me a 10:00 wake. Still nine "แบบวันที่ 3".
9. The "next unlock" hint changes every time I look: "อีก 1 Night ritual…" → "อีก 19 ครั้ง 10 นาทีแรก…" → "อีก 9 งาน Must…". It feels random, not like a goal.
10. At bedtime the card says **"ไปนอน"** and right under it **"ถัดไป: นอน · 23:30"**. Next is… the same thing?
11. The workout sheet defaulted to **30 นาที** even though I'd planned 60 for today. It could pre-fill from the plan.
