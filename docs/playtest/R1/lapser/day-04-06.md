# Days 4–6 — Fri → Sun · lapser (gone)

App opened: **never.** 0 taps.

## Why I stopped
- **Fri (day 4):** The coffee-shop client sent a wall of comments at 07:30. I woke up straight into LINE, worked in bed, and forgot the "ตื่นแล้ว" button. By 10:00 I'd already "failed" the morning (no Sunrise ticket anyway), so opening the app felt pointless. I'd only see "เริ่มช้า" on everything. I worked until 02:00 and skipped the Night ritual because I was fried.
- **Sat (day 5):** Friend's birthday and a late night out. I thought about the pig once ("is it hungry?") and decided I'd "start fresh Monday". Classic.
- **Sun (day 6):** Slept until noon. Honestly, I was a bit scared to open it: weeds, a broken streak, a hungry pig. Avoiding the guilt was easier than facing it. Out of sight, out of mind. The icon is just one of many on my iPad, and nothing reminded me it existed.

## What could have pulled me back
1. **One gentle notification on Fri morning** saying something like *"หมูยังอิ่มอยู่ ไม่ต้องรีบ กดตื่นแล้วตอนไหนก็ได้"* ("the pig is still full, no rush, press I'm-up whenever"). It needs to say lateness is OK, *not* "your streak is at risk". (No push exists yet, so nothing reached me at all.)
2. **A "busy day" mode I could have set in 2 taps on Friday:** "today is deadline day, just track wake + sleep". Then a crazy day doesn't count as a fail. The streak sheet mentions "วันลาพักและวันป่วยจะหยุดนับไว้ก่อน", but I never saw where to set a leave day, and I didn't know "busy" was allowed.
3. **Knowing that coming back is cheap.** If I had known "skip 3 days = the pig gets sleepy but nothing dies", I'd have opened it on Saturday. Not knowing what damage was waiting is what kept me away.
4. A home-screen widget or lock-screen glance at the farm. Seeing the pig would have been enough of a nudge.

Would I have opened it on any of these days without a reminder? **No.** That's the honest answer.
