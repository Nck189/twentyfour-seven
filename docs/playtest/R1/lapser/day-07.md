# Day 7 — the comeback · lapser

Screenshots: `day07-return.png` (first screen on return), `day07-farm.png`, `day07-evening.png`

## First reaction on opening (08:29, after 3 days gone)
I opened it braced for a scolding. What I got was… **nothing.** The Today screen looks exactly like any normal morning: the big green "ตื่นแล้ว — ลุกจากเตียง · เป้าหมาย 08:00", the Wake block, and "0 จาก 3 สัปดาห์นี้". There's no "welcome back" and no "you missed 3 days". The only hint is the streak bubble, which quietly went from 1 to **0**. A new tab, "สถิติ", has appeared in the bottom bar.

- **Does it welcome or scold me?** Neither. Relief first ("phew, no guilt trip"), then a little deflated, like walking into a room where nobody noticed I'd been away. One line like *"กลับมาแล้ว! หมูคิดถึง"* would have made me smile.
- **Restarting:** it's technically easy, because the normal day is right there. I pressed "ตื่นแล้ว" and the very first thing it told me after 3 days away was **"ตื่น 08:29 บันทึกแล้ว รอบนี้ไม่ได้ตั๋ว"**. Then the Wake block said "เริ่ม 08:29 · เริ่มช้า". Two "not good enough"s in my first 5 seconds back. Oof.

## Streak
The streak sheet: "ติดต่อกัน 0 วัน · สูงสุด 1 วัน", and the calendar has 1 green square followed by **4 brown "พลาด" squares**. The first brown one is **day 3, my best day ever** (pig hatched, prism strawberry, every block done). It's brown only because I pressed "ตื่นแล้ว" at 08:21. Meanwhile, Stats › รายวัน › "วันไหนทำได้ดี" shows that same Friday as my greenest day. Two screens disagree about whether I tried. The two scarecrows next to "หุ่นไล่กาช่วยรักษาสตรีคในวันที่พลาด ได้ทุก 7 วัน" look like I own two, so why didn't they save me? (I think they're empty slots, but they look the same as owned ones.)

## Farm and animals (what 3 days did)
- **Every plot has weeds.** Tapping one: "มีวัชพืชจากวันที่พลาด เลยหยุดโตก่อน จะหายหลังผ่านวันขั้นต่ำ (ตื่นตรงเวลา + Night ritual)" plus "เริ่มเหี่ยว: พลาด 3 วันติด คุณภาพลด 1 ขั้น". This is clear and fair: it tells me how to fix it.
- **The Down Time turnip is dead:** "ตายแล้ว เพราะพลาด 5 วันติด". I was gone 3 days, not 5. Then I realised that plot never grew even on my keen days, because I always did the Night ritual but never pressed "เริ่ม" on Down Time. (See below: you basically *can't* press it from Today.) So the one plant that died was killed by doing the ritual "wrong". The replant was easy: tap plot → pick seed, 2 taps.
- **The pig:** a sleepy pig with zzz bubbles and five empty hearts: "ง่วงและหิว ออกกำลังกายครั้งเดียวทุกตัวอิ่ม 2 วัน". Honestly, this was the **nicest part of the comeback.** It isn't dead or angry, just sleepy, and it tells me exactly what fixes it. I fed it ("อิ่มเพิ่มอีก 1 วัน"), petted it ("หมู ชอบมาก!"), and got a surprise **"+1 เห็ดทรัฟเฟิล"**. I went for a run at lunch: "บันทึกแล้ว! สัตว์ในฟาร์มอิ่มไปอีก 2 วัน". Pig is happy, I'm happy.
- My decorations (lantern, fence, mailbox, flowers) were all still there. Coins: 10, no change. Truffle and harvest values still don't turn into anything I can see.

## Good
- **No guilt trip.** Nothing red, no "you failed", no "your streak died!!" modal. The pig sheet is the gold standard: it names the state and tells me the one action that fixes it.
- Weeds/wilt explain themselves and say exactly what clears them (one minimum day).
- Stats › การนอน: "นอนตื่นสม่ำเสมอแค่ไหน · สม่ำเสมอมาก ±5 นาที". That's nice to read after a lapse. It focuses on what I *did*.
- I got my first "ตรงเวลา" on a daytime block (Work 10:32).

## Bad / confusing
1. **No acknowledgment of the return at all.** The app behaves as if yesterday was normal. It's not punishing, but there's no "welcome back, here's your 1-minute restart" either. I had to discover the dead plant, the weeds and the sleepy pig by tapping around.
2. **The first sentence back is "รอบนี้ไม่ได้ตั๋ว".** 29 minutes "late" on a comeback morning. A returning player should get the Sunrise ticket for just showing up.
3. **I can't start Down Time from Today.** When Down Time is the current block, the Today card is replaced by "ถึง Down Time แล้ว พร้อมปิดวันนี้และวางแผนพรุ่งนี้ไหม? · เริ่ม Night ritual". No "เริ่ม", no first-10. I had to open "ดูทั้งวัน" → tap "Down Time" → "เริ่ม" inside a sheet. Its first-10 is literally "เล่นมือถือได้ 10 นาที → เริ่ม Night ritual", so the ritual *is* Down Time, yet doing it doesn't count, and that's why my turnip died.
4. **Day 3 shown as "พลาด"** in the streak calendar while the heatmap shows it as my best day.
5. Stats overview "คะแนนวัน · 7 วันล่าสุด · 4%". My keen days' bars are barely taller than my absent days'. 4% feels like a slap, and it doesn't look right for days where 3 blocks are green in the heatmap.
6. By night the timeline showed **ตื่น / โฟกัส / งาน / Gradation / เวลาว่าง all "กำลังทำ" at once.** I did tap "จบบล็อกนี้" on Work at 19:40, but it still said กำลังทำ afterwards.
7. The capsule gave me a turnip, the exact starter that just died, labelled "ใหม่!". Not new; a bit cruel, actually funny.
8. Exercise still "1 จาก 3 สัปดาห์นี้" though the pig counted my run; the week counters keep confusing me.

## Was it easy to restart?
**Mechanically yes (~2 minutes, ~10 taps to fix the farm). Emotionally, mostly OK.** The pig and the weed texts made it feel recoverable. What hurt was the small stuff: no ticket on the first tap, "เริ่มช้า", a 4% score, a brown square on my best day, and a dead plant I didn't understand. None of it is a scolding, but it adds up to *"you're behind"*. I'd come back again if something reminded me; I wouldn't come back on my own.

## Will I open it tomorrow? (0–10) — **6**
The pig is full for 2 days and I want those weeds gone (one clean wake + ritual). But the streak is 0, the score is 4%, and nothing on screen says "nice to have you back". If I sleep in tomorrow and miss the ticket again, I could easily drift off for good.
