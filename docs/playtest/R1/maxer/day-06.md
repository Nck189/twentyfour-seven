## How the app reacted

- **Pressing "I'm up" at 10:45 gave me *no feedback at all*.** The big button vanished, with no "Up at 10:45" line, no "late", no "no ticket". Compare day 7, when I was only 17 min late: "Up at 08:47. Logged — no ticket this time." Today, 2h+ late, it was simply silent. I'm not sure it registered.
- **Today's weather on the Farm said "Sunrise Glow · Woke on time" and "Spark · Exercised"** on the day I woke at 10:45 and didn't exercise. Either it's yesterday's weather still showing, or the weather is wrong. Either way, it rewards a lazy morning with a "woke on time" badge. (Screenshot `day06-late-wake.png`; the weather chips are in the right pane.)
- No nudge during the day. (No push yet, so that's expected.) In-app, the whole-day list just turned every block to **"Missed"** by 23:46: Wake, Focus, Work, Gradation, Free. Neutral wording, no shaming, no red banners. Calm.
- The streak header still read **4** all day. It doesn't drop until the next morning.
- I didn't do the ritual, so the Down Time prompt just sat there. No guilt-trip copy. Good.

## Was it fair?
The consequences showed up the next morning (see day 7):
- Streak **4 → 0**. The rules say "A scarecrow saves your streak on a missed day. You earn one every 7 days (up to 2)". I'd only had a 4-day streak, so no scarecrow yet. That's fair by the printed rules. But the streak dialog *shows two scarecrow icons* the whole time, so I assumed I had two banked. **That felt like being lied to**, even though the text was technically right.
- **0 tickets** for the day (no Sunrise, no Night). Fair, and it's exactly the price I expected.
- Weeds on Free (and all the missed plots), so growth is paused. Fair-ish; it's what the plot dialog warned.
- **The Focus Radish died: "It died after 5 misses in a row."** Five misses = days 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 of a **0-minute block** I could never do. The lazy day was just the fifth strike. Not fair: it would have died this week even if I'd played perfectly.

Overall the lazy day is **mild and honest**: you lose that day's two tickets and your streak, and nothing is clawed back. I can live with that. The only unfair bits were the template-made Focus death and the misleading scarecrow icons.
