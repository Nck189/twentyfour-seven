## Notes, in my own words

- **Good:** sign-in with a code is painless. Setup is honestly short. "Use all suggestions" is a one-tap skip, which I like. But it *instantly* jumped to "4 / 4 All set" without showing me what it picked for each block. I had to trust it.
- **Setup doesn't save mid-way.** I navigated away at step 3 by accident and came back to "1 / 4 What's your week like?" with everything gone. Minor, but annoying.
- **Wake target mismatch.** After setup, Plan says *Today · Work day · Wake 08:30*, but the big Today button still says **"Target 10:00 · Tap the moment you leave the bed."** Which one counts for my Sunrise ticket? Maybe today's plan was frozen before setup. I'll check tomorrow.
- **Setup rewards checklist ("Set up 24/7", 7 items) is the best part of the evening for me.** Concrete numbers: "Add a checklist to a block · 20 coins", "Add a task with a time estimate · 15 coins", "Choose your language · 5 coins", "Set your weekly exercise goal · 3 bags of animal feed", plus wake times → "2 Strawberry seeds" and first-10 → "1 capsule ticket". I cleared 6 of 7.
- **7th reward is impossible for me:** "Add 24/7 to your Home Screen · Flowers for your farm". The More screen only says "Install on iPhone: In Safari, tap Share, then 'Add to Home Screen'". I'm on a Mac. There's no desktop instruction and no way to claim it, so I'm stuck at **6 of 7** forever. For a completionist that's the worst kind of number.
- **I typed the task "Review regression test plan 45m !must tomorrow #work" and the screen did nothing.** The backlog still said "Nothing here yet." It turns out "tomorrow" sent it to Wed's plan, but there was no toast, so I thought the button was broken and typed it again.

- **Farm on desktop:** the right-hand pane on the Farm page says **"Backlog — Everything you need to clear will live here. The backlog is coming soon."** But the Plan page has a working Backlog with my task in it. Contradiction.
- The farm sky was bright midday blue with a sun at 21:00 and 23:40. Cute, but wrong.
- **Plots:** "It grows each day Work goes well: started on time, or 70% of its checklist." That's a clear rule, I like it. Growth time only shows in the screen-reader label ("Work: Strawberry, day 0 of 2"). Visually there's nothing: no days-to-harvest, no yield, no sell value. Starter seeds (Radish, Turnip, Lettuce, Carrot) are free and unlimited. I put the 2 Strawberries in Wake and Work, my two most reliable blocks.
- **Capsule machine:** "Rare+ in 10 · Epic+ in 30 · Legendary by 80". Visible pity, nice! But **no base odds anywhere.** I pulled my single ticket to learn the system: **"Radish · Common · New!"**. A free starter seed, the one I could already plant for nothing. The counter went to "Rare+ in 9 · Epic+ in 29 · Legendary by 79", so singles count toward pity. - Streak dialog explains the rules clearly: "Keep it: press 'I'm up' in time and do your Night ritual. Perfect day: also a Day score of 80% or more (+1 ticket)." And "A scarecrow saves your streak… You earn one every 7 days (up to 2)." Love that it's spelled out.
