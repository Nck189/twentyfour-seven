- Results:
  - **anon**: `submit_weekly_review`, `weekly_review_status` and `select weekly_reviews` are all permission denied.
  - **Direct writes**: authenticated insert/update/delete on `weekly_reviews` are denied. Inserting `weekly_review_done` / `days_settled` events violates RLS, while a plain client type (`tip_seen`) is still allowed. Ledger insert is RLS-denied. `update game_state` → 0 rows. `delete day_results` is denied. `_review_week` is not callable.
  - **Validation**: 281 chars, whitespace-only and NULL are rejected. The value is trimmed and stored.
  - **Once per week**: first call `granted:true` (+3), edit `granted:false` with content updated, still 3 tickets and 1 ledger row. **6 parallel submits** from another user: exactly one `granted:true`, tickets 3, 1 ledger row.
  - **User B** can't see A's review, and B's status is independent.
  - **Window**: a 3-hourly clock sweep from Sat to Wed (BKK, 10:00 wake) gives `_review_week` non-null exactly from Sunday's wake to Tuesday's wake, with week_start = that Monday.
  - **Timezone trick** on Tuesday afternoon (to Pago Pago, making the wake-day Monday again) is refused by the M9 guard, and the review stays closed. **Template trick**: moving the target wake to 23:30 after it passed is frozen by the snapshot, so the wake-day stays Tuesday.
  - **New user** signing up on Sunday gets +3 for the previous week (R-M10-6).
- **Grandfather update**: a fresh DB `g` with migrations up to M9 and 5 users, then M10 applied alone. `wake_snapshots` and `events` counts unchanged, only `updated_at` bumped, `tips_seen` set to all 8 ids.

**TS⇄SQL streak parity:**
- 7 patterns of 88–330 days:
  - 198 straight good days;
  - (8 good + 2 missed)×12;
  - 35/65/65 runs with breaks;
  - 61 good, a break, 61 good;
  - freezes and a Sick day mixed in;
  - two seeded random sequences of 320–330 days with G/M/S.
- Each was driven day by day through real `settle_days` on the mocked clock: wake_event + night grant inserted as service_role, then settle the next morning.
- The `days_settled` grants and the final `game_state` were compared with `settleStreak` run via tsx on the same sequence. **All 7 match exactly**: same final streak/best/freezes, same grant dates and lengths, e.g. `[7,14,30,60,90,120,150,180]` and restarts `[…150, 60, 90]`.
- Repeated `streak_30` grants land on distinct `ref_date`s, so there are no ledger collisions. `best_streak` can't be lowered by the client.
- Algebraically, restarting never pays more than keeping the streak: total ≤ 2+⌊N/30⌋ either way.

**Stats math:**
- I-MR hand-checked against the unit-test sample: mean 442.2, MR̄ 49.2, σ 43.6, UCL 573.1, and 630 flagged.
- No divide-by-zero paths: `imrChart` needs ≥2 points; Pareto/home-vs-out/regularity guard empty sets; `sigma=0` flags nothing.
- Excused/planned/backfilled/open days are handled through `blockResult`/`dayOee` (Vacation/Sick have null OEE; backfilled is not judged; planned deviation is excluded from Availability).
- Only finished days are used (`date < today`), bedtime wraps at noon, and the profile timezone is used.
- Pre-signup days are the defect (R-M10-1, reproduced with the real modules).
