# G7 independent review — M9 (streaks, seasons, unlocks)

**Commit range:** `5042028..46384a5` (branch `m9-local`). I started at `d20058a`. During the review three commits landed: `00cad60` (prettier), `bad5881` (service_role bypass in the past-day guards) and `46384a5` (docs). I reviewed those too and re-ran every exploit below on a fresh scratch Postgres 16 built from `46384a5`.

**Verdict: FAIL** (1 × S1, 4 × S2 open)

## Summary

What works:
- **Parity.** The settle engine matches the TS rules. Over 25 random users × 40 days (misses, Vacation/Sick, late nights, planned/backfilled logs), `settle_days` = `settleStreak`, and `_day_oee` = `dayOee`, with 0 mismatches.
- **Ledgers.** They are read-only and pay once. 6 parallel settles paid exactly one `streak_7` and one `streak_14`.
- **Locked down.** Internal functions, reward-only decor and forged `days_settled` events are all closed to clients.
- **Setup rewards.** They still pay after the `_setup_reward` / `_grant_unlock` redefinitions.

What fails:
- **The streak can be had without the Night ritual.** The "planned late night" stand-in is forgeable after the fact (S1). By design, it also counts as a full streak day (S2).
- **Past days can still be excused.** A timezone hop does it (S2).
- **Yesterday's Perfect day can be forged the next morning** (S2).
- **The Must unlock counts the current priority**, so relabelling done tasks pays it (S2).

## What I verified and how

- **Scratch Postgres 16 with all migrations (twice).** First at `d20058a`, then a fresh database at `46384a5` (port 55443) where every S1/S2 above was re-run. I acted as `authenticated` with `request.jwt.claim.sub`, as `service_role` and as `anon`.
- **RLS and grants.**
  - `day_results` and `unlock_grants` are select-only with forced RLS.
  - `game_state`, `ticket_grants`, `coin_grants` and `inventory` have select policies only.
  - `decor` has a column-level update on `x,y` only. An insert is refused by RLS, and `update item_id` gets permission denied.
  - `events` refuses `days_settled`.
  - As `authenticated`, `_grant_unlock`, `_day_oee`, `_unlock_counts`, `_festival_month` and `_decor_price` all get permission denied. `anon` can't run `settle_days` or `claim_unlocks`.
- **Decor.** `buy_decor('krathong')` is allowed in November and `star_tree` is refused ("not in season"). `garden_lantern` and `mailbox` are refused ("unknown item").
- **Parity (TS ⇄ SQL).** A `tsx` generator (scratchpad `par/`) ran 25 random users × 40 days: wake/ritual 80%, Vacation/Sick 6%, late nights 12%, logs with on-time/first-10/items, 8% planned and 5% backfilled. I compared `day_results` kinds, OEE (±1e-3), streak/best/freezes and `perfect_day` / `streak_*` grants against `settleStreak`: **0 mismatches**. The run covered 16 frozen days, 17 × `streak_7`, 2 × `streak_14` and 69 Perfect days.
- **Races and re-settles.**
  - 6 concurrent `settle_days` over 15 good days gave exactly one `streak_7` and one `streak_14`, tickets 10, streak 15 and freezes 2 (the 2-freeze cap held).
  - A second settle pays nothing, and `day_results` can't be written or deleted by the client.
  - Moving `profiles.created_at` (the client can) only changes where the first settle starts. Data before sign-up doesn't exist, so it gains nothing.
- **Guards.**
  - Past Vacation/Sick is refused directly, a past planned tag is refused, and a late night with a `return_at` more than 1 h in the past is refused. The `{}`, `now()` and timezone-hop bypasses are in R-M9-1 and R-M9-3.
  - Moving the wake via a template edit is blocked by the wake snapshot.
  - After `bad5881`, `service_role` can again seed past Vacation, planned tags and late nights, and users are still refused. `current_setting('role')` remains the caller's role inside the definer function, and users can't `set role service_role`.
- **Setup rewards after the redefinitions.** `claim_setup_rewards` still pays `sleep_times`. `claim_unlocks` with best_streak 30 paid `streak_7` (an egg, a fed duck) and `streak_30` (a pond).
- **Wake-day boundary.** By code, `settle_days` settles while `d < _wake_day`. Before today's wake it stops at D−2, and the Night ticket window (≤ next wake − 1 h) closes before that.
- **Unit tests, lint and E2E listing.** `npx vitest run tests/unit` passes 248/248, `npm run -s lint` exits 0, and `npx playwright test --list tests/e2e/streak.spec.ts` lists 18 tests (3 × 6 projects). I checked the E2E selectors against the components and `en.json`: "Streak: 7 days", "7 days in a row", "Unlocked!", "Collection", "First steps", "30 more", "This month: …" and "This month" all match the real names. Their weaknesses are in R-M9-12.
- **UI, i18n and art.**
  - EN/TH keys are at parity (no missing keys either way). There is no Thai or emoji in the new code, and no hex or black colours in the new art (tokens and `TONES` only, 2–3 tones).
  - The screenshots `m9/m9-art.png`, `collection.png` and `farm-top.png` look on-style. Locked silhouettes are a soft grey (`brightness(0) invert(.6)` at 40%), not black.
  - Chips and buttons are ≥ 44 px (`h-11` / `min-h-11`).
  - Feature reveals fail open (`?? true`, error → shown) before the migration, except R-M9-8.
