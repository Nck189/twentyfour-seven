# Growth plan — how 24/7 could reach many people

Minnie, 2026-11-03: "คิดวิธีให้แมส ละจดไว้ก่อน อันไหน include ระหว่างทำไปเลยได้ก้ทำ".
Ideas that fit a planned milestone are built **as part of that milestone** (column "When"); the rest wait for after v1.
Rule for all of them: rewards and anything social stay server-checked (SPEC §0.6), no emoji, pictures over words.

## The one number that matters first
**2-week retention** of a pilot group (10–20 friends): how many still press "I'm up" + do the Night ritual on day 14.
≥ 50% = strong; < 25% = fix the first week before anything else. Measured from the `events` table (D1 / D7 / D14 / D30 cohorts), never from UI state.

## Ideas
| # | Idea | Why it helps | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Easy first week:** day 1 shows only Wake + Night ritual; other blocks unlock one by one (ties into the unlock ladder §8.8b and progressive disclosure) | most habit apps lose people in week 1 because there's too much to do | M9 (unlocks) + M14 (onboarding) |
| G2 | **Setup rewards** (§8.8c) incl. Home Screen install | people finish setup, the app lives on the Home Screen | M7 |
| G3 | **Retention dashboard for Minnie** (cohorts from `events`: D1/D7/D14/D30, where people drop off in the day) | know what to fix with data, not guesses (PDCA) | M8 (stats) |
| G4 | **Share card:** a pretty weekly picture (farm + streak + Day OEE ring, no private data) to save/share to IG/TikTok stories | free marketing; people love showing a cute farm | M8 (weekly review) |
| G5 | **Invite a friend:** invite link (also the invite-only sign-up noted in PROGRESS); both get a seed capsule when the friend finishes their first minimum day (server-checked, once) | growth through friends; reward only real use | M7 (economy) + invite system |
| G6 | **Visit a friend's farm** (read-only) and send a small gift (water/fertilizer) once a day | social pull to open the app daily | v2 (share links) |
| G7 | **Wake buddy:** pair with a friend, both see if the other pressed "I'm up" on time; a shared streak | accountability is the strongest habit lever | v2 |
| G8 | **Kinds of day presets by audience** (student, office worker, shift worker, parent) in onboarding | first plan fits in 1 minute for more people | M14 (onboarding) |
| G9 | **Thai seasons & festivals** (Loy Krathong, Songkran, New Year) as limited farm events | reasons to come back + timely social posts | M9 (seasons §8.8) |
| G10 | **Gentle, well-timed reminders** (Down Time, wake window) with one-tap actions; quiet by default | brings people back without being annoying | M10 (push) |
| G11 | **In-app feedback** (one tap, optional screenshot) during the pilot | fast learning loop | M8 (small) |
| G12 | **Guest try-out:** a demo farm on the landing page before sign-up | lowers the first step | after v1 |
| G13 | **App Store / Play Store** wrapper (e.g. Capacitor) for discovery and reliable iPhone push | people find apps in stores | after v1 |
| G14 | **Landing page + story content:** "an IE student runs her life like a factory (OEE, PDCA) and grows a farm" — timelapse of a farm growing from real days | a story people share; Minnie's angle is unique | after v1 (Minnie) |
| G15 | **Privacy promise in plain words** (data is yours, export anytime, no ads) | trust for a life-tracking app | M14 (polish) |

## Not now
Ads, paid features, public leaderboards (cheating pressure + privacy) — revisit only after the pilot shows people stay.
